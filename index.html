Score mechanics

Measure → rescale 0–1 → weight by swing → add up → shake. The hub in focus is worked through below.

1 · MeasureOne real datapoint per factor, with source and year.
2 · Rescale 0–1Best of 66 = 1, worst = 0; ranks and wages flipped so higher is better.
3 · Weight by swingWorst-to-best on one factor: how much better? Most valuable move = 100 points; reason written beside each.
4 · Add upScore = Σ weight share × rescaled value. Missing factors drop out.
5 · ShakeEvery weight ±50 %; five robustness variants; lead-share = how often a hub leads.

Keys to success for a business of this type

Sell control and predictability, not tokens

Tokens cost cents and are paid by the customer to a third party; prices fall 5–40×/yr at fixed capability. Durable claims: data custody, review-once determinism, no metering surprises.

research note 17 I-1, P-1

Certifications before exploration spend

SOC 2 Type II / ISO 27001 / ISO 42001 are the gate regulated buyers apply and the DORA/OSFI entry ticket.

research note 15; competitor_analysis.md §6.2

Fund the eval set first; adapters are disposable

The curated pairs and held-out eval survive every base-model change; an adapter dates with each release (3–4-month lag).

research note 17 I-4; research notes 03, 09

Model-agnosticism as the exit option

Capability gains arrive free; so do threats. Worth most in Scenario C, least in A. Budget 0.05–0.1 FTE model maintenance.

research note 16

Price on capacity bands or sites, never per token or per head

Value accrues at the free refresh; cost at rare authoring. Per-seat penalises the roll-out where value is created.

research note 17 P-2, P-5

Measure k and the cache-hit rate in the first pilot

The two unmeasured numbers that decide the local-vs-cloud TCO ordering.

research notes 11, 12, 14

Treat retrieved content as untrusted

LLMs do not enforce a boundary between instructions and data (NCSC). Read-only credentials, sandboxed code, 12–24-month activity record.

research note 15

Contextual ambidexterity around one shared asset

A five-person firm runs exploration with the same people through a gated loop; the eval set is both the exploitation gate and the exploration instrument.

Technical sensitivities that can reorder the leaders

Retry multiplier k

If ≥ 6 % of local-model requests cost one analyst-minute of retry, the Sonnet API is cheaper than local at any size (k* = 1.06; vs Haiku 1.003). k is unmeasured: first pilot metric.

research note 11 · artificialanalysis.ai

Prefix caching

Sonnet per-call $0.020 → $0.0124 with a 4k cached prefix (−38 %); a 32k schema $0.074 → $0.0151; TCO break-even moves out ×1.6. Local GPUs get no relief (128k KV ≈ 34 GB on a 27B model).

research note 12 · developers.openai.com

Retries compound

3 agent steps at p = 0.9 → 3.3 calls; p = 0.5 → 6; p = 0.3 → 10. Cloud TCO up 2–3× under retries; ranking vs on-prem unchanged.

research note 14

Fine-tune cost and cadence

≈ $158 per 150M-token LoRA re-tune on a 27B base (Together list); 3–4×/yr at the open-weight lag. Self-serve fine-tuning withdrawn at OpenAI, Mistral, Cohere; none at Anthropic.

Model lifetimes

research note 16 · epoch.ai

DORA and localisation

DORA has no general EU data-localisation mandate: Art. 28 register and exit strategy, Art. 30 processing locations and audit rights. OSFI B-10: no blanket keep-data-in-Canada rule.

research note 15 · eur-lex.europa.eu

Energy

IEA 2026 update ≈ 950 TWh data-centre demand by 2030 (supersedes 945). NovaStorms local ≈ 6 Wh/request; idle server ≈ $118/yr at Halifax 13.43 ¢/kWh. Electricity spread among Canadian hubs < $600/yr: second-order.

research note 07 · www.iea.org

Evaluation is the asset

BIRD top 82.95 % vs human 92.96 %; Spider 2.0-DBT 65.6. The eval set survives every base-model change; adapters do not.

research note 09 · bird-bench.github.io

Memory wall

8B model ≈ 128 KiB KV per token (16 GiB at 128k); 95.5 tok/s (8B int4, RTX 4090) vs 13.6 tok/s (70B int4). Decoding is memory-bound.

research note 05

Quantum

Not in a five-year horizon: 48 error-corrected logical qubits (Quantinuum Helios, Nov 2025); IBM target 200 by 2029. One exploitation item: post-quantum crypto readiness.

research note 10
  • Edge AI / code-once. Model called at authoring only; reviewed code re-run locally. Why: economics independent of token prices. Limit: authoring quality depends on the model the customer brings.
  • LoRA adapter. Small extra weights on a frozen open-weight base; ≈ USD 158 per re-tune. Why: closes a narrow gap when air-gapped. Limit: dates with each base release; assumed, not confirmed by NovaStorms.
  • Evaluation set. Held-out customer questions with reviewed answers. Why: judges any model; survives churn. Limit: none exists yet for NovaStorms' task.
  • Prefix caching. Stable prompt prefix re-read at 0.05–0.1× price. Why: flattens the schema penalty for API users. Limit: local GPUs pay in memory.
  • Retry multiplier k. Calls made ÷ ideal calls. Why: decides local vs API cost. Limit: unmeasured.
  • DORA register vs localisation. Register, processing locations, audit, exit; no localisation mandate. Why: corrects a premise in the buyer factor. Limit: text read from a mirror.
How AI was used

Research notes were drafted with AI and critiqued in a separate pass; the framing note records where the team's intuitions were corrected. Humans set the questions, factors and weights; weights change only by dated decision. The instructor's condition held: you need to know what to ask.
